# Household Services (Haushaltsnahe Dienstleistungen)

## What is it?

Haushaltsnahe Dienstleistungen are services performed in or around your home. You can deduct **20% of the labor costs** directly from your tax liability (not from taxable income - this is better!).

## Rates for 2025

- **20% of labor costs** (not materials)
- **Maximum deduction: €4,000 per year**
- This means maximum eligible labor costs: €20,000

## What Qualifies?

### Cleaning & Maintenance
- House cleaning services
- Window cleaning
- Garden maintenance (mowing, hedge trimming, snow removal)
- Gutter cleaning

### Personal Care Services
- Home care for elderly or sick family members
- Meal delivery services (labor portion only)

### Other Services
- Pet care at your home
- Laundry services
- Moving services (within Germany)
- Chimney sweeping

## What Does NOT Qualify

❌ **New construction** - Only existing homes
❌ **Materials and supplies** - Only labor costs deductible
❌ **Services outside your home** - Must be at your residence
❌ **Business/commercial use** - Only private households

## Critical Requirements

### 1. Must Be Paid by Bank Transfer
**CASH PAYMENTS ARE NOT DEDUCTIBLE!**

You must pay via:
- Bank transfer (Überweisung)
- Direct debit (Lastschrift)
- Debit/credit card

### 2. Labor Must Be Itemized
The invoice must **separate labor and materials**:
```
Example Invoice:
Materials: €100
Labor: €200
Total: €300

→ Only €200 × 20% = €40 deductible
```

### 3. Service Must Be at Your Home
- Your primary residence (where you live)
- Vacation home in Germany also qualifies
- Landlord services count if you paid via Nebenkosten

## How to Calculate

**Formula:**
```
Labor costs × 20% = Tax reduction
(Maximum: €4,000)
```

**Example:**
- House cleaning: €120/month × 12 = €1,440 labor costs
- Deduction: €1,440 × 20% = **€288 tax reduction**

## Renters Can Claim Too!

If you're a renter, check your **Nebenkostenabrechnung** (utility bill) for:
- Hausmeister (building superintendent)
- Gartenpflege (garden maintenance)
- Treppenhausreinigung (stairwell cleaning)
- Schornsteinfeger (chimney sweep)

Calculate your share based on your apartment percentage.

## What You Need

### Required Documents
1. **Detailed invoice** showing:
   - Your name and address
   - Service provider details
   - Date of service
   - **Labor costs separated from materials**

2. **Proof of payment**:
   - Bank statement showing transfer
   - Invoice marked "paid via bank transfer"

3. **For renters**: Nebenkostenabrechnung from landlord

### Do NOT Send with Tax Return
- Keep documents for **audit purposes only**
- Only submit if Finanzamt requests (Nachforderung)

## Tax Form Location

**Main Form (Mantelbogen)**
- Lines 71-74: Household services
- Enter total labor costs (ELSTER calculates 20%)
- Do NOT include materials

## Combining with Handwerkerleistungen

You can claim BOTH:
- Haushaltsnahe Dienstleistungen: max €4,000
- Handwerkerleistungen (craftsmen): max €1,200
- **Total possible: €5,200 tax reduction**

## Common Examples

### House Cleaning
- **Service**: Weekly cleaning, €30/visit × 50 weeks = €1,500
- **Payment**: Bank transfer monthly
- **Deduction**: €1,500 × 20% = €300

### Garden Maintenance
- **Service**: Lawn mowing, hedge trimming, €500/year labor
- **Materials**: €100 (not deductible)
- **Deduction**: €500 × 20% = €100

### Renter - Utilities
- **Hausmeister in Nebenkosten**: €240/year (your share)
- **Treppenhausreinigung**: €120/year
- **Total labor**: €360
- **Deduction**: €360 × 20% = €72

## Common Mistakes

❌ **Paying cash** - Not deductible, must be bank transfer
❌ **Including materials** - Only labor costs qualify
❌ **No itemization** - Invoice must separate labor from materials
❌ **Forgetting Nebenkosten** - Renters often miss this!
❌ **Exceeding €20,000** - Only first €20,000 labor costs count (= €4,000 max deduction)

## Pro Tips

✅ **Ask service provider** to itemize labor separately on invoice
✅ **Always pay by bank transfer**, never cash
✅ **Keep all invoices** for 2 years after tax assessment
✅ **Check your Nebenkosten** if you rent - free money!
✅ **Regular services** (weekly cleaning) are easier to document
