# Commuting Deduction (Entfernungspauschale)

## What is it?

The Entfernungspauschale allows employees to deduct commuting costs from their taxable income. This applies to the distance from your home to your first workplace.

## Rates for 2025

- **First 20 kilometers**: €0.30 per kilometer
- **From 21st kilometer onwards**: €0.38 per kilometer
- **Maximum deduction**: €4,500 per year

## How to Calculate

1. Measure the **one-way distance** from home to work in kilometers
2. Count your **workdays** (typically 230 days minus vacation days minus home office days)
3. Multiply: distance × workdays × rate
4. If over 20km, calculate first 20km at €0.30, rest at €0.38

**Example:**
- Distance: 25 km
- Workdays: 200 days
- Calculation:
  - First 20 km: 20 × 200 × €0.30 = €1,200
  - Remaining 5 km: 5 × 200 × €0.38 = €380
  - **Total: €1,580**

## Important Rules

- Only **one-way distance** counts (not round trip)
- Use the **shortest route** (exceptions for faster routes with good reason)
- Both **car and public transport** qualify for the same rates
- If you work from home, reduce workdays accordingly
- **Public transport season tickets** can be deducted at actual cost if higher than the flat rate

## What You Need

- **For ELSTER**: Enter distance and workdays in Anlage N (lines 31-39)
- **Proof**: Keep records of your work address and home address
- **No receipts needed** for the flat rate (Pauschale)

## Special Cases

### Home Office Days
If you work 150 days from home and 150 days in office:
- Commuting deduction applies only to office days (150 days)

### Multiple Workplaces
- Deduction applies to your **regular first workplace**
- Business trips to other locations have different rules (Dienstreise)

### Job Change Mid-Year
Calculate separately for each job:
- Job 1: distance × days worked at Job 1
- Job 2: distance × days worked at Job 2
- Total must not exceed €4,500

## Tax Form Location

**Anlage N (Employment Income)**
- Line 31: Distance in kilometers
- Line 32: Number of workdays
- ELSTER automatically calculates the deduction
