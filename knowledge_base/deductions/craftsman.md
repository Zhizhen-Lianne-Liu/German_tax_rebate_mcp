# Craftsman Services (Handwerkerleistungen)

## What is it?

Handwerkerleistungen are craftsman services for **renovation, maintenance, and modernization** of your existing home. You can deduct **20% of the labor costs** directly from your tax liability.

## Rates for 2025

- **20% of labor costs** (not materials)
- **Maximum deduction: €1,200 per year**
- This means maximum eligible labor costs: €6,000

## What Qualifies?

### Repairs & Maintenance
- Plumbing repairs (leaky faucet, pipes)
- Electrical work (outlets, lighting, wiring)
- Heating system maintenance and repairs
- Roof repairs
- Window and door repairs
- Painting and wallpapering
- Flooring repairs

### Renovations & Modernization
- Bathroom renovation
- Kitchen remodel
- Installing new windows/doors
- Insulation improvements
- Solar panel installation
- Heat pump installation

### Outdoor Work
- Paving driveway or walkways
- Fence repairs or installation
- Garage door repairs

## What Does NOT Qualify

❌ **New construction** - Only existing buildings
❌ **Expanding living space** - No additions or extensions
❌ **Materials** - Only labor is deductible
❌ **Off-site work** - Craftsman must work at your home
❌ **DIY materials** - You must hire a professional

## Key Difference from Household Services

| Haushaltsnahe Dienstleistungen | Handwerkerleistungen |
|-------------------------------|---------------------|
| Regular maintenance (cleaning, gardening) | Repairs & renovations by craftsmen |
| Max €4,000 deduction | Max €1,200 deduction |
| Service-oriented | Skilled trade work |

**You can claim BOTH!** Maximum total: €5,200 tax reduction per year.

## Critical Requirements

### 1. Labor Must Be Itemized
Invoice must **separate labor, materials, and machine costs**:

```
Example Invoice:
Materials: €500
Labor: €800
Machine costs (can include): €200
Total: €1,500

→ Deductible: (€800 + €200) × 20% = €200
```

### 2. Payment by Bank Transfer (Preferred)
While cash is technically allowed, **bank transfer is strongly recommended**:
- Bank transfer = clear audit trail
- Cash = Finanzamt may question it
- Best practice: Always pay by transfer

### 3. Work Must Be at Existing Property
- Your primary residence
- Second home in Germany
- Rental property you own
- Does NOT include new construction (Neubau)

## How to Calculate

**Formula:**
```
(Labor + Machine costs) × 20% = Tax reduction
(Maximum: €1,200)
```

**Example:**
- Bathroom renovation invoice:
  - Materials (tiles, fixtures): €2,000 ❌
  - Labor (plumber, tiler): €3,500 ✅
  - Machinery rental: €500 ✅
- Deductible costs: €3,500 + €500 = €4,000
- Deduction: €4,000 × 20% = **€800 tax reduction**

## What You Need

### Required Documents
1. **Detailed invoice** with:
   - Your name and address
   - Craftsman's business details
   - Description of work performed
   - **Labor separated from materials**
   - Date of service

2. **Proof of payment**:
   - Bank statement (preferred)
   - Receipt marked "paid"

3. **Betriebsstättenprinzip**:
   - Work must be done at your property
   - If craftsman takes item to workshop, only on-site labor qualifies

### Do NOT Send with Tax Return
- Keep for audit purposes (2 years after assessment)
- Only submit if Finanzamt requests

## Tax Form Location

**Main Form (Mantelbogen)**
- Lines 75-77: Craftsman services
- Enter total labor + machine costs
- ELSTER calculates 20% automatically

## Common Scenarios

### Plumbing Repair
- **Problem**: Leaky pipe, water damage
- **Invoice**: Labor €400, materials €100
- **Deduction**: €400 × 20% = €80

### Heating System Service
- **Annual maintenance**: Labor €150
- **Deduction**: €150 × 20% = €30

### Bathroom Renovation
- **Labor**: €5,000
- **Materials**: €3,000 (not deductible)
- **Deduction**: €5,000 × 20% = €1,000

### Kitchen Remodel
- **Labor**: €8,000
- **Materials**: €12,000
- **Deduction**: Only first €6,000 labor = €6,000 × 20% = **€1,200 (maximum)**

## Special Cases

### Renters
Even renters can claim if:
- Landlord requires you to pay for repairs
- You paid for improvements with landlord's permission
- You have invoice and proof of payment in your name

### Rental Property Owners
If you rent out property:
- Repairs are **business expenses** (Werbungskosten)
- Do NOT use Handwerkerleistungen deduction
- Claim in Anlage V (rental income) instead

### Emergency Repairs
- Same rules apply
- Common: burst pipes, heating failure in winter
- Keep invoice showing emergency surcharge

## Combining Multiple Services

You can combine different craftsmen in one year:

**Example Annual Total:**
```
January: Plumber (€400 labor)
March: Electrician (€600 labor)
July: Painter (€2,000 labor)
October: Heating maintenance (€150 labor)

Total eligible labor: €3,150
Deduction: €3,150 × 20% = €630
```

## Common Mistakes

❌ **Including materials** - Only labor + machines qualify
❌ **Claiming new construction** - Must be existing building
❌ **No itemization** - Invoice must separate costs
❌ **Exceeding €6,000** - Only first €6,000 labor counts
❌ **Forgetting small repairs** - Every invoice adds up!

## Pro Tips

✅ **Get itemized invoices** - Ask craftsman to separate labor clearly
✅ **Plan major renovations** - Spread over 2 years if >€6,000 labor
✅ **Keep all invoices** - Even small €50 repairs count
✅ **Always pay by bank transfer** - Easier to prove
✅ **Check if Haushaltsnahe qualifies instead** - Higher max (€4,000 vs €1,200)

## Haushaltsnahe vs. Handwerker Decision

**When in doubt:**
- **Repair/renovation by skilled craftsman** → Handwerkerleistungen
- **Regular service or maintenance** → Haushaltsnahe Dienstleistungen
- **Example**: Gardener building fence → Handwerker
- **Example**: Gardener mowing lawn → Haushaltsnahe
