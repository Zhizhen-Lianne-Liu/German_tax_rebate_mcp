# Home Office Deduction (Homeoffice-Pauschale)

## What is it?

The Homeoffice-Pauschale is a flat-rate deduction for employees and self-employed individuals who work from home. Introduced to simplify home office deductions during COVID-19, it remains available.

## Rates for 2025

- **€6 per day** worked from home
- **Maximum 210 days** per year
- **Maximum deduction: €1,260** per year (210 × €6)

## Eligibility

You can claim this deduction if you:
- Work from your home (apartment, house)
- Do your work activities there (not just check emails)
- Have no other workplace available that day

## How to Calculate

**Simple Formula:**
```
Days worked from home × €6 = Deduction
(Maximum: 210 days × €6 = €1,260)
```

**Example:**
- Worked 150 days from home in 2025
- Deduction: 150 × €6 = **€900**

## Important Rules

- **No receipts needed** - it's a flat rate
- **No proof of actual costs** - you don't need to document internet bills, electricity, etc.
- **Cannot combine** with dedicated home office room deduction (choose better option)
- Applies to **both employees and self-employed**
- Only days where home was your **primary workplace** count

## Dedicated Home Office Room (Alternative)

If you have a **dedicated room** used **exclusively** for work, you might get a higher deduction:
- Calculate: (room size / total apartment size) × (rent + utilities)
- **Example**: 15m² office in 75m² apartment, rent €1,500/month
  - Deduction: (15/75) × €1,500 × 12 = €3,600/year
- **Requirements**:
  - Room must be separate (not kitchen table)
  - Used **exclusively** for work (no personal use)
  - Center of your professional activity

**Choose the higher amount!** Most people benefit from the simple €6/day rate.

## Combining with Commuting Deduction

**You can claim BOTH:**
- Days in office: Claim commuting deduction (Entfernungspauschale)
- Days at home: Claim home office deduction (Homeoffice-Pauschale)

**Example:**
- 150 days in office (20km commute): 150 × 20 × €0.30 = €900 commuting
- 150 days at home: 150 × €6 = €900 home office
- **Total: €1,800**

## What You Need

- **Count your home office days** throughout the year
- **Keep a calendar** or log (not required but helpful if audited)
- **For ELSTER**: Enter days in Anlage N, line 43

## Special Cases

### Hybrid Work
Many employees work 2-3 days at home, 2-3 days in office:
- Just count the days at home
- Maximum 210 days applies

### Multiple Jobs
The 210-day maximum applies **per person**, not per job.

### Self-Employed
Same rules apply. Enter in Anlage EÜR or Gewinnermittlung.

## Tax Form Location

**Anlage N (Employees)** or **Anlage S (Self-Employed)**
- Line 43 (Anlage N): Number of home office days
- ELSTER automatically calculates: days × €6

## Common Mistakes

❌ **Claiming 365 days** - Maximum is 210 days
❌ **Claiming weekend days** - Only actual workdays count
❌ **Claiming vacation days** - You didn't work from home on vacation
❌ **Forgetting to subtract office days** - Only home days qualify
